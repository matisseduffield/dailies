Shunt

How to play

Slide the cars and trucks aside until the yellow car, the one with the arrows, can leave through the gap in the wall.

Slide the blue car down to clear the way
1

Drag a car or truck along its length. It goes as far as the way is clear, and a slide of any length is one move.

2

Par is the fewest moves it can possibly be done in. Take as many as you need: the closer to par, the better.

3

Undo and Restart put cars and trucks back, but a move made stays spent.

Par climbs through the week, from 18 on Monday to 30 or more on Sunday.
